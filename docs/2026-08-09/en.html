The Low Frequency Trap: Video Language Models Fail at Simple Event Bookkeeping

Researchers have discovered that video language models fail at simple event bookkeeping due to a low frequency trap, which matters because it affects their ability to understand and describe what is happening in a video. This occurs because real-world video benchmarks have fixed clips that entangle event count, rate, duration, and visual complexity, making it hard to isolate failure modes. To address this, the researchers introduced a trace-grounded parametric profiling method for event counting in three controlled video tasks.
