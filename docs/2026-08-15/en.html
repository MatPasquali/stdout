V-RAE: Rethinking Video Latent Spaces for Generation

Researchers have proposed V-RAE, a video representation autoencoder that builds compact generative latent spaces on top of pre-trained vision model representations, allowing for better high-level semantic organization, which is crucial for video generation, as latent spaces optimized for pixel-level reconstruction are not necessarily well-suited for generative modeling. This matters because latent video generation relies on autoencoders to define a compact space in which generative models operate. The V-RAE includes a lightweight temporal pooling module that removes temporal redundancy while preserving semantic structure, and a video decoder that reconstructs continuous motion from the compressed features. The V-RAE results are evaluated with four representative frozen encoders.
