GradCuit: Credit-Assigned Gradient Flow Enables Robust and Interpretable Test-Time Latent Reasoning

Researchers introduced GradCuit, an approach that enables direct and interpretable credit assignment in latent reasoning processes for large language models, improving robustness and the ability to explain decisions made during the optimization of continuous states at test time. This is achieved by inserting optimizable latent states at a selected Transformer layer, allowing causal self-attention to provide a differentiable connection between the log-probabilities of each continuation token and the preceding latent states.
