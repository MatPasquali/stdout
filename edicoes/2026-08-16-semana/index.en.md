### [guillaumemeyer/watermarks-remover](https://github.com/guillaumemeyer/watermarks-remover)
`GitHub`

A project on GitHub, called watermarks-remover, was created to remove artificial intelligence provenance marks from multiple vendors, which matters because these marks can be used to track the origin of images and texts.
