`arXiv`

A new approach to vision-language-action (VLA) models has been introduced on arXiv, which matters because these models are common in robotics and artificial intelligence. The TurboVLA model reformulates the traditional processing pathway, which typically involves converting visual data into linguistic representations before being translated into robot actions, into a more direct approach that combines vision and language to predict continuous actions. This is achieved through a process of independently encoding visual observations and language instructions, followed by lightweight bidirectional vision-language interaction. TurboVLA is capable of operating in real time at 32 Hz on high-end hardware like the RTX 4090, using less than 1 GB of VRAM.
