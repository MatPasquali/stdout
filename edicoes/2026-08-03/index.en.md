### [TokTier: Exact Stateful Tokenization for Agentic LLM Serving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29678v1)
`arXiv`

Researchers have developed TokTier, a stateful tokenization service that aims to improve the efficiency of large language model (LLM) serving systems, which are used in artificial intelligence applications. This matters because current tokenization systems can be inefficient, especially when it comes to long dialogue sessions, as they re-tokenize the entire request text on every call.
