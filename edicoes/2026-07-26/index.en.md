### [Beyond Sufficiency: Time Series Explanation with Counterfactual Necessity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21573v1)
`arXiv`

Researchers have developed a new approach to explaining time-series classifiers, called TimePNS, which goes beyond the notion of sufficiency and incorporates counterfactual necessity, a concept based on the idea that a temporal factor is necessary if intervening on it disrupts the model's prediction, which is crucial for understanding how machine learning models make decisions. This matters because faithful explanations should identify subsequences that not only preserve the model's prediction, but also are necessary to maintain it. TimePNS is a two-stage framework that learns an identifiable causal generative process and a sufficiency-oriented explanation mask. 
The new approach can be useful for avoiding the assignment of importance to spurious subsequences that support the prediction without being essential to the model's decision.

### [Beyond Sycophancy: Structured Resistance and Compliance in LLM Moral Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21558v1)
`arXiv`

Researchers published a study on arXiv about developing large language models that can learn from others without simply acquiescing to their opinions, which is crucial for creating socially calibrated models. This requires models to distinguish when to incorporate others' perspectives and when to maintain a well-grounded moral judgment. The study found that models' judgment revision is structured along three dimensions that parallel classic phenomena in human social psychology: the distance between an incoming view and the model's initial position, the source attribution of that view, and the coalition structure supporting it. Models are generally more receptive to nearby positions and more influenced by views presented as their own.

`arXiv`

This matters because on-policy self-distillation is a promising approach to improving model learning, but it typically requires a teacher with more advanced knowledge. VCSD achieves this by converting image content removal into a self-distillation signal, allowing the student to learn from its own responses.
